# Governance

Forebay is young enough that pretending to have elaborate governance would be theatre. This document
says what is actually true today and what it will become, so nobody has to guess.

## Today

The project was started by [@mayur-tolexo](https://github.com/mayur-tolexo), who is currently the
sole maintainer and makes the final call on design and merges.

That is a weakness, not a feature, and it is written down here so that it is visible rather than
implied. A single-maintainer project is a bus factor of one, and the goal is to stop being one.

## Decisions

Design decisions are made through RFCs in [`docs/rfcs/`](docs/rfcs/README.md).

Anyone may open an RFC. Discussion happens in the pull request. An RFC is accepted when the
maintainers agree it is ready and open questions have been answered or explicitly deferred with the
deferral recorded in the document.

Disagreement is settled by argument, and evidence outranks seniority. A measurement beats an
opinion, an opinion grounded in production experience beats an unsupported preference, and the
maintainer breaking a tie has to say why in writing. If a decision later proves wrong, the RFC is
superseded rather than quietly edited, so the record of what was believed and when survives.

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no quota and no timetable. Maintainers are added by invitation from existing maintainers
after a sustained pattern of good judgement, typically shown through RFC review and design
discussion rather than commit count. Reviewing someone else's design carefully is the clearest
signal available, which is why it weighs more here than volume of code.

Maintainers may step down at any time and are moved to emeritus. This is normal and carries no
stigma.
